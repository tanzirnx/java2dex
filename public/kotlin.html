Good to know

          
    
            
  • Up to 20MB per file, 50 files per run — or upload a .zip and it's auto-extracted.
    • 
            
  • Android SDK classes (android.app.*, android.widget.*, etc.) compile out of the box.
    • 
            
  • Kotlin stdlib functions compile and dex fine, but the stdlib itself must be bundled in your final APK to run — see Help.
    • 
            
  • Files are deleted from the server right after your download starts.
    • 
            
  • Successful and failed runs are saved to History in your browser.
    • 
            
  • Ctrl+Enter to convert, Esc to clear the queue.
